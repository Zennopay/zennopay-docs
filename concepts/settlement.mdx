---
title: "Reconciliation"
description: "Recover the authoritative state of QR payments and Platform Payouts."
---

Both Zennopay products execute asynchronously. A successful create or confirm
response proves that an operation was accepted, not that destination delivery
is final. Your backend should reconcile every operation from durable local
records, signed webhooks, and authoritative status reads.

## Product-specific sources of truth

| Product | Correlation keys | Authoritative read | Webhook reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| QR Payments | Intent ID and `reconciliation_ref` | `GET /v1/payment_intents/{id}` | [QR Payments webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks) |
| Platform Payouts | Payout ID and your `reference` | `GET /v1/payouts/{id}` | [Payout webhooks](/platform-payouts/webhooks) |

Each linked product reference owns its lifecycle states and event types. This
page defines the shared recovery behavior.

## Reconciliation rules

1. Persist your business operation and idempotency key before calling the API.
2. Store each webhook before returning `2xx`, then deduplicate it by event ID.
3. Expect duplicate and out-of-order webhook delivery.
4. After a timeout or ambiguous response, retrieve the existing resource or
   retry the same create request with the same idempotency key.
5. Treat the resource returned by its `GET` endpoint as current truth.
6. Escalate resources that remain non-terminal beyond your operating window;
   never invent a terminal state locally.

## Keep products separate

Do not join a QR payment and a Platform Payout merely because they use the
same partner reference. Their identifiers, statuses, destinations, balance
movements, and webhook event types are independent. Store the product type
alongside every external ID in your ledger or operations table.

## Environment isolation

Sandbox and live resources are separate. Include the environment in local
uniqueness constraints and reconciliation jobs so a sandbox ID, event, or
reference cannot affect live accounting. See
[Environments](/api-reference/environments).
