Zennopay provides two payment products behind one server-to-server trust model.
Choose the flow that matches what your application needs to move.

## QR Payments

Embed a native PaymentSheet that scans a supported merchant QR, validates it
server-side, confirms the wallet debit once, and tracks local merchant delivery
asynchronously.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrate PaymentSheet" icon="mobile" href="/payments/overview">
    Pick iOS, Android, Flutter, or React Native and present the complete native
    checkout flow.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Platform Payouts

Send an asynchronous bank payout from a prefunded balance. Store or inline a
beneficiary, create an idempotent payout, then poll or consume signed webhooks
until its terminal state.
